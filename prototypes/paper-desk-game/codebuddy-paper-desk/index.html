How to play

  1. You are running a simulated one-person investment bank with $100,000.
  2. Each market day starts with three AI agents: Macro, Research, and Risk.
  3. Use the sliders or plus/minus buttons to allocate exactly 100% across NVDA, AMD, TSLA, BTC, and SPY.
  4. Click Run market day. The market resolves and your portfolio is scored against SPY.
  5. Repeat for five days. A good run beats the benchmark while keeping drawdown controlled.

Demo story

  1. Start conservative: leave capital in SPY and read Day 1's AI infra thesis.
  2. Move some capital into NVDA or AMD if you trust the Research Agent.
  3. On macro scare days, reduce concentrated risk and compare your PnL with the benchmark.
  4. By Day 5, the question is not “did AI predict everything?” It is “did your process create auditable decisions?”
  5. StockWin connection: this game is the playful version of a serious idea: paper-test AI market theses before real trading.
